# Automotive guidance

Use one documented vehicle variant. Establish wheelbase, track, wheel diameter and
axle heights first; label visually estimated measurements. Check wheel-center positions
and front/rear overhang in aligned side and front views before body details.

Model primary body volumes as low-density symmetric surfaces. Read the subdivision
skill when building smooth panels. Inspect curvature across panel boundaries, wheel
arches, pillars and roof transitions. Preserve intentional panel gaps. Separate glass,
lamp housings and lenses; tinted surfaces are not substitutes for missing structure.

Require approved silhouette and wheel placement before secondary panel work. Validate
neutral reflections and orthographic views. Tire contact, steering clearance and panel
manufacturability need explicit scope and evidence. BMA provides this workflow guidance;
it has no verified automatic vehicle reconstruction or curvature-continuity solver.

# Character guidance

Establish anatomical landmarks or an explicit stylized proportion chart. Approve the
primary silhouette in neutral front, side and perspective views before facial detail.
Preserve approved identity features; do not replace an existing sculpt with generation.

Place edge loops around joints and facial movement regions according to intended
deformation. Check shoulder/hip volume, elbow/knee bending, mouth corners and eyelids
in actual test poses when rigging is in scope. Clean quads alone do not prove rig readiness.
Inspect normals, self-contact, weights and rest-pose symmetry with the rig team or user.

Use the subdivision profile for the cage where appropriate. BMA does not automate
anatomical diagnosis, weight validation or pose sweeps. Report rigging readiness as
unverified unless an actual deformation test was performed and inspected.

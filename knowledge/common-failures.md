# Failure patterns

| Symptom | Cause hypothesis | Targeted strategy | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Correct details, wrong outline | Details began before proportions were approved | Return to blockout ratios within authorized scope | Aligned silhouette and dimension comparison |
| Bevel disappears at corners | Width exceeds local spacing; clamp overlap may engage | Inspect clearance; reduce only the affected bevel or adjust local geometry | Close-up plus evaluated topology report |
| Dark surface islands | Winding, overlapping faces or smoothing settings | Inspect source incidence and normals; correct only the demonstrated cause | Report samples and neutral views |
| Cloth floats or cuts through support | Scale, collision or contact assumptions | Inspect contact section and actual simulation frame | Images; no inferred collision-free claim |
| Fine mesh replaces useful original | Unapproved rebuild or remesh | Restore checkpoint, plan scoped edits | Preserved object fingerprints and explicit authorization |

These are recommendations and hypotheses, not proven solutions to a particular asset.
Promote a lesson only after its specified validation is actually performed.

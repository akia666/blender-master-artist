# Modeling priorities

Work from silhouette and proportions through major forms, secondary forms, functional
details, topology, materials and presentation. Fix the highest-impact evidenced error
first. A polished shader cannot compensate for a wrong shape.

Preserve user geometry, dependencies and useful edge flow. Local edits are preferable
when they satisfy the requested change. A rebuild can be justified, but requires scoped
authorization and a rollback checkpoint. Maintain moving parts separately and expose
uncertainty about scale or construction instead of inventing precision.

All geometric estimates have a coordinate convention, source and uncertainty. A neutral
view and technical validation answer different questions and are both required.

For the decision framework, domain limits and reproducible techniques read
[modeling principles](../references/modeling-principles.md) and
[risk-based QA](../references/quality-assurance.md). The
[anonymized case](../case-studies/cyberpunk-megacity.md) separates observed outcomes
from recommendations; editorial notes are not automatically verified runtime records.

# Reference matching

Read the reference-analysis skill. Inspect available licensed images, classify views,
record the design variant and uncertainty, and establish dimensions without false
precision. Reference absence is a limitation, not an excuse to invent specifications.

Align datum, projection, crop and scale. Compare blockout dimensions and silhouettes
before secondary geometry. Use binary-mask IoU only for aligned segmented views and
report the alignment/segmentation method. Look at the actual images for depth and
mechanical construction questions that occupancy cannot answer.

Record discrepancies as measurable ratios or observations tied to a named view.
Correct the largest approved-scope error, capture new images and rerun checks.
Do not turn a user's preference for one asset into a universal rule.

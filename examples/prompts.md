# Example requests

**New asset:** Use Blender Master Artist and official Blender Lab MCP to design a
240 × 160 mm removable-lid enclosure. Treat those dimensions as a synthetic design
specification, preserve all existing objects, and present the research and blockout
stages for explicit approval before refining them.

**Existing asset:** Inspect the selected asset and its dependencies without modifying
it. Report source/evaluated counts and defects with sample indices. Propose a scoped,
non-destructive correction plan and preserve every approved component.

**Reference match:** Inspect the supplied licensed front and side references. Establish
coordinates, label estimated dimensions, compare the blockout ratios and outline,
and explain camera/scale uncertainty before changing the geometry.

**QA only:** Run the game profile on the specified mesh names and capture four neutral
views. Report errors, warnings and unverified export requirements. Do not repair,
apply modifiers, remesh or approve a stage.
